Privacy Policy for EasyApps

Effective Date: 23rd of November, 2025
Last Updated: 23rd of November, 2025

1. Introduction​

Welcome to EasyApps ("we," "us," or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our Discord bot services, including our Normal Bot and White-Label Bot services (collectively, the "Service").

By using the Service, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the collection, use, and disclosure of your information as described herein.

2. Information We Collect​

2.1 Information Collected from All Users​

Discord Server Information​

When you add our bot to your Discord server, we may collect:

  • Server ID
  • Server name (for operational purposes)
  • Channel IDs where the bot is active

Configuration Data​

When you run /server_setup or configure the bot, we collect and store:

  • Server ID
  • Configuration settings you provide
  • Channel assignments
  • Role configurations
  • Any custom settings you specify

Application Data​

When you create and use application features, we collect:

  • Server ID
  • Log channel ID
  • Application name and configuration
  • Application templates and questions

When an application is submitted, we store:

  • Server ID
  • User ID (of the applicant)
  • Message ID
  • Application responses

Operational Logs​

We collect operational logs, including:

  • Command usage logs
  • Error logs and debugging information
  • Bot activity logs
  • Performance metrics
  • Timestamps of bot interactions

2.2 Additional Information from White-Label Customers​

When you create a White-Label account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Account settings and preferences

Payment processing is handled by Stripe. We do not store your complete payment card information. Please refer to Stripe's Privacy Policy for more information.

3. How We Use Your Information​

We use the collected information to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Execute bot commands and features
  • Process application submissions and workflows
  • Diagnose and resolve technical issues
  • Analyse usage patterns and trends
  • Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service

4. Data Storage and Retention​

All data is stored on secure servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information.

  • Configuration Data: Retained while your bot remains active
  • Application Data: Retained indefinitely unless you request deletion
  • Operational Logs: Retained indefinitely unless you request deletion
  • Bot Tokens (White-Label): Deleted within 30 days of service termination

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at support@easy-apps.xyz.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure​

We may share information with:

  • Discord: To provide bot functionality (Discord's Privacy Policy)
  • Stripe: For White-Label payment processing (Stripe's Privacy Policy)
  • Hosting providers: For secure server infrastructure

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Your Rights and Choices​

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of non-essential communications

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@easy-apps.xyz.

7. European Privacy Rights (GDPR)​

If you are located in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you have additional rights under GDPR including the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, and the right to object. Contact us at support@easy-apps.xyz to exercise these rights.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)​

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under CCPA. We do not sell personal information. Contact us at support@easy-apps.xyz to exercise your rights.

9. Children's Privacy​

Our Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us at support@easy-apps.xyz.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy​

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. We will provide at least 7 days' notice of material changes. Your continued use of the Service after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

11. Contact Information​

Email: support@easy-apps.xyz
